Step 7 Selection Phase

Create Your Timeline Anchors

Looking back from the life beyond your anchor point, remember the signs and signals that told you your new timeline was coming into form.

What This Step Is

In this step you are going to go back to that place 3 to 6 months beyond your anchor point, where everything has already shifted and the new timeline is simply your life. From that place, you look back at the journey and remember the signs and signals you noticed along the way. The things that kept showing up and telling you that your new reality was coming into form.

This matters because your subconscious moves toward what you program it to recognize. When you name these signs from the felt memory of having already lived them, you are not waiting for proof. You are recalling it. And your subconscious does not distinguish between a vivid memory and the present moment. That is the power of this exercise.

You are not watching for something to happen. You are remembering that it did.

Before You Write

Take a breath and step back into that place beyond your anchor point. The shift has happened. You are settled into the new timeline. Now look back over the journey that got you here. What did you notice along the way that told you things were changing? Let that memory come forward before you write.

Part One: Evidence Anchors

Looking back from beyond your anchor point, make a list of the signs and signals you noticed that told you your new timeline was becoming real. Think about what started showing up in your inbox, your bank account, your conversations, your body, and the kinds of opportunities that came your way. These are the breadcrumbs you followed. List as many as come to mind.

Looking back from beyond your anchor point, what signs and signals told you that your new timeline was becoming real? What started showing up that was different from before?

Part Two: Your Random Confirmation Sign

Now add one piece of evidence that is so random, so unexpected, that seeing it in your daily life could only mean one thing. Looking back from beyond your anchor point, this was the sign that stopped you in your tracks. The one that made you smile because you knew. Your higher self and your spirit team found ways to put it in front of you because you had made it important. You had put it on the list.

For example: blue feathers. Not something you would normally spot on a walk or in your neighborhood, but one day a logo, a truck, a storefront showed it to you. And you knew.

Looking back, what was the unexpected sign that kept appearing to confirm you were on the right path? The one that felt too specific to be a coincidence.

Part Three: Visual Timeline Anchor

Create a visual that represents the life beyond your anchor point. This is the image of that settled, shifted reality you have been stepping into throughout this phase. It can be a Canva image, a journal drawing, a phone wallpaper, or a vivid mental picture you can call up instantly. It does not need to be elaborate. What matters is that when you see it, you feel that place.

Use this image first thing in the morning and right before you go to bed. Anytime your mind begins to drift or doubt starts creeping in, call up this image and return to that feeling of already being there.

Describe your visual anchor. What does it look like? What does it feel like when you see it? How will you make it accessible, on your phone, in your journal, or as a mental image you can summon?
