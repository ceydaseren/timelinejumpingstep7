Step 7 Selection Phase

Create Your Timeline Anchors

Signs, signals, and visual evidence that confirm your new timeline is already coming into form.

What This Step Is

In this step you are going to create your timeline anchors: the signs and signals you would expect to experience on the timeline you are selecting. These are things that could come up in your day to day that tell you your new reality is coming into form.

This is about calibrating your perception and training your brain to look for confirming evidence. Where your focus goes, energy flows. You are programming your subconscious to understand that these signs are important to you so that your energy begins to direct itself toward finding them.

You are not waiting for proof. You are choosing what to look for. And by looking for it, you create the conditions for it to show up.

Before You Write
Take a breath and bring your new timeline to mind. Feel into it for a moment. What does that reality feel like? Let that feeling guide what you write below.
Part One: Evidence Anchors

Make an itemized list of signs and signals you would expect to see on your new timeline. Think about what would show up in your email inbox, in your bank account, in conversations, in your body, or in the kinds of opportunities that come your way. Keep this list handy and revisit it daily or weekly. You can always add to it.

What signs or signals would tell you that your new timeline is becoming real? List anything that would look or feel different from your current reality.

Part Two: Your Random Confirmation Sign

Add at least one anchor that is so random you would not ordinarily expect to see it. When this sign appears in your life, you will know without question that your timeline is locking in. Your higher self and spirit team will find ways to put it in front of you because you placed it on the list and made it important to you.

For example: blue feathers. Not something you would normally spot on a walk or in your neighborhood, but one day a logo, a truck, a storefront might show it to you. And you will know.

What is your random confirmation sign? Choose something unexpected, something that would make you stop and smile if it appeared.

Part Three: Visual Timeline Anchor

Create a visual that represents your new timeline. This can be a Canva image, a journal drawing, a phone wallpaper, or a vivid mental picture you can call up instantly. It does not need to be elaborate. What matters is that you can pull it to mind easily.

Use this image first thing in the morning and right before you go to bed. Anytime your mind begins to drift or doubt starts creeping in, call up this image and redirect your energy toward what you are building.

Describe your visual anchor. What does it look like? What does it feel like when you see it? How will you make it accessible, on your phone, in your journal, or as a mental image you can summon?
